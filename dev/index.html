BoxDMK.PeriodicGrid — Type
PeriodicGrid

Lattice and level-0 geometry of the periodic forest. The cell {A t : t ∈ [0,1)³} (lattice vectors = columns of A) is handled in the rotated frame x_rot = Qᵀ x with A = Q U, U upper triangular with a positive diagonal (C++ BdmkLattice). The level-0 grid has mgrid[d] cubes of side rc covering the bounding box of the cell from xgrid0, plus nhalo image layers: mle = mgrid .+ 2nhalo boxes per axis from xelo = xgrid0 .- nhalo * rc.

BoxDMK.PeriodicPlan — Type
PeriodicPlan

Forest and precomputation of bdmk_periodic for repeated solves on a fixed forest (e.g. time stepping): plan = periodic_plan(f, cell; kwargs...), then periodic_solve!(plan, f) for each new density (the first solve uses the samples taken by the plan). The result of a solve shares the plan's buffers (pot, fvals), which the next solve overwrites. The forest is adapted to the density given to periodic_plan; later densities must be resolved by it.

BoxDMK.PeriodicResult — Type
PeriodicResult

Output of bdmk_periodic: the forest ft, its level-0 grid, the density samples fvals and potential pot (both (n, n, n, nboxes) on each box's Legendre grid), and istarget (false on image-halo boxes, whose potential is not computed). Evaluate anywhere with evaluate_periodic.

BoxDMK.PotentialField — Type
PotentialField(tree, result)

Attach a SolverResult to its BoxTree so the solved potential (and, when computed, gradient/hessian) can be evaluated at arbitrary points of the domain [0, boxlen]^ndim.

The field is callable: field(x) evaluates the potential at a point x (returns a scalar for nd == 1, a length-nd vector otherwise), and field(X) evaluates at each column of a (ndim, ntarg) matrix. Use evaluate_potential, evaluate_gradient, and evaluate_hessian for output with explicit (nd, ...) shapes.

Evaluation locates the leaf box containing the point by descending the tree, then evaluates the box's tensor-product polynomial interpolant.

BoxDMK.bdmk_periodic — Method
bdmk_periodic(f, cell; eps=1e-6, norder=16, rc_factor=0.25, nhalo=1, f_batch=nothing,
              skip_negligible=true, interpolate=true, timings=nothing)

Periodic Coulomb potential

u(x) = ∫_cell Σ_n f(y) / |x - y - A n| dy        (k = 0 mode dropped)

of the cell-periodic density f(x::AbstractVector) -> Real. cell is the 3×3 matrix A of lattice vectors (columns), a 3-tuple L for the orthorhombic cell [0, L₁) × [0, L₂) × [0, L₃), or a number for a cube. A density with nonzero mean is taken with a uniform neutralizing background (the k = 0 mode of the whole potential is dropped). f_batch!(values, points) (values (1, npts), points (3, npts)) may be given instead of f. The level-0 boxes have side rc = d_min / ⌈1/rc_factor⌉, d_min the smallest distance between lattice planes. With skip_negligible boxes whose density is too small to change the potential by eps relative are left out of the real-space part (see _source_boxes). With interpolate (cells whose level-0 cubes do not tile the cell) only the boxes meeting the cell sample f, the others interpolate them at periodic images; false gives the forest of the C++ builder. targets (3 × N points) restricts the computation to the leaves holding them and returns the potential there in target_pot. timings (length ≥ 6) receives wall times (s): [1] forest, [2] setup and wide source, [3] local expansions + upward pass, [4] wide part, [5] plane waves, [6] near field, and if length ≥ 10 the plane-wave split [7] formation, [8] translation, [9] evaluation, [10] downward pass.
